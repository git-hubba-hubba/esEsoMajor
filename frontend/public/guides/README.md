# Guide portraits

Andi, Maya, and Leo use distinct fictional human portraits generated with the built-in imagegen tool, resized to 384px and served locally. Guide selection, signup, dialogs, and local walkthroughs all use the shared GuidePortrait component. No generation or live-video request occurs when displaying a portrait.

## Saved assets and final prompts

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square human account-guide profile portrait for a telecom dashboard. Subject: Andi, a fictional approachable adult in their thirties, short wavy dark brown hair, warm light brown skin, navy casual shirt. One fictional human only, friendly relaxed smile, looking at camera. Natural photographic headshot, full head and upper shoulders centered with generous margins for circular cropping; head occupies about 55% of image height. Pale silver-blue plain studio background, soft daylight, natural skin texture, consistent professional yet welcoming styling. No mascot, cartoon, headset, props, logos, text or watermark.
